One room. Four agents. Four humans.

Switch is the coordination layer, not a notification afterthought. The work happens in the room.

01

Extract & evidence

Scout splits the ask into atomic claims and attaches dated sources. An assertion in chat is not evidence.

02

Assess

Actuary applies deterministic policy and writes narrowed commitment language. No LLM decides a number.

03

Challenge

Examiner recomputes economics, checks evidence age, and blocks the packet by default. A block forces a new hash.

04

Approve & seal

Clerk records exact human phrases, seals a signed receipt, and hands only approved commitments to delivery.
