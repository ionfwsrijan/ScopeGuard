Two rooms. Four agents. Four signers.

Switch is the coordination layer, not a notification afterthought. The work happens in the room.

01

Scout: evidence

Scout turns the client's message into four typed asks and attaches dated, sourced evidence to each. An assertion in chat is not evidence.

02

Pricer: the number

Pricer applies deterministic policy and writes priced, narrowed language. Same engagement, policy, and evidence always produce the same total.

03

Red Team: the attack

Red Team recomputes the margin, checks autonomy and evidence age, and blocks the packet by default. A block forces a new hash and invalidates old signatures.

04

Clerk: the signature

Clerk records exact human phrases, seals a two-party change order, and hands only signed line items to the linked delivery room.
